The Mascot

Meet Breezy.

A banana. Sunglasses on. Stick in hand. Sneakers laced. Breezy doesn't do warmups, doesn't do small talk, and has never once lost a ground ball. He showed up, went bananas, and never left.

The Walk-On

Stick over the shoulder, shades on, already late for face-off. Breezy walks like he owns the field — because he does.

The Badge

The official mark. Small on the chest, big on the back. If you know, you know — and if you don't, ask the guy in the banana tee.

The Lounger

Game over, feet up, stick as a recliner. Breezy rests exactly as hard as he plays. Which is to say: dramatically.

The Thumbs-Up

Breezy approves of your shot selection. He does not approve of your defense. He will tell you both to your face.

Our Story

Two brothers.
One very serious banana.

Banana Lacrosse started with two brothers, a love for the game, and an idea that lacrosse apparel could have a little more personality.

We wanted to create something that felt bigger than just another piece of team gear.

Something you could wear to practice, to class, on the weekend, or anywhere in between.

Vintage-inspired style. Lacrosse at heart. A banana who refuses to take himself seriously.

Welcome to Banana Lacrosse.

The brand.

Banana Lacrosse is vintage-style lacrosse apparel. Heavyweight tees, washed hoodies, small-logo athletic wear, rope hats and the original Breezy plush — made for players, and for everyone who wishes they still were. Fun, a little cocky, never childish. For players 12 to adult.

The banana.

Breezy is the banana. Sunglasses on, sneakers laced, lacrosse stick in hand. Small on the chest, big on the back — and when Breezy goes big on the front, there's only one thing it can say: GO BANANAS.

Drop 001.

Drop 001 is the first lineup — 51 pieces. Tees, hoodies and long sleeves, athletic wear, shorts, jackets, rope hats and the original plush, plus one mystery: the Rare Galaxy Breezy Plush, which only ever shows up with the Galaxy Tee. Join the Drop List for first access.
